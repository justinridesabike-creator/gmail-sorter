Gmail Sorter

A personal inbox-organizing tool.

Gmail Sorter is a private tool used only by its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer and files incoming email into the owner's existing Gmail labels, learning from how the owner has sorted mail before. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

Privacy policy

What it accesses: message metadata in the owner's Gmail account, specifically the sender, subject line, mailing-list headers and labels. It does not read attachments.

What it does with it: it decides which label a message belongs in, then applies that label or archives the message in the owner's own Gmail account. It cannot permanently delete email.

Where data is kept: only on the owner's computer (a small model of sender patterns and a log of actions) and in the owner's Gmail account. Nothing is sent to, sold to or shared with any third party, and Google user data is not used for advertising.

Removing access: access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
